Vegetarian and vegan

A whole menu of its own

GAM GAM offers a wide choice of vegetarian and vegan main courses and delicacies, kept separate from the meat and fish dishes. The fresh and organic produce of the Venice morning markets makes it easy to serve food that is good to eat and good for you.

We take the environment seriously: recycling in every area, take-away materials made from recycled products, low-energy lighting, and sustainable, organic and locally grown food wherever we can.

Shabbat

If you are travelling in Europe, this is the place to be

All year round, Chabad of Venice offers Shabbat for visitors and for the community: candle lighting together, services in the Yeshiva synagogue in the Ghetto, and then a Shabbat table where you will find someone who speaks only French, someone who speaks Hebrew, someone who speaks only English — and after HaMotzi on the challah, everyone is united. In the high season some Shabbatot have drawn around eight hundred people.

Shabbat meals are by reservation only, through jewishvenice.org — not through the restaurant. Take-away is also available.
